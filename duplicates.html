Safety rules

This tool cannot delete a whole group by accident.

  • Every group must keep at least one record. The delete button refuses to empty a group.
  • Names are compared ignoring case, spacing, punctuation and leading list numbers.
  • Truncated names match their full form, including middle cuts like Sse...ongo → Ssentongo.
  • Contacts are compared using the last 9 digits, so +256 700 123456 matches 0700123456.
  • A backup file is downloaded automatically before anything is deleted.
